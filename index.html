I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in robotics and artificial intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), affiliated with the Multi-Scale Medical Robotics Center in Hong Kong SAR. I received my Ph.D. from the Bioinspired Robotics and Medical Technology (BMT) group at CUHK, advised by Prof. Samuel Au.

My current research interests include:

  • Safety-aware autonomous surgical systems
  • Deformable object modeling, planning, and manipulation
  • In-hand object reconstruction and manipulation

Open-Source Projects

  • Virtual RCM: QP-based remote center of motion control for general-purpose robot arms.
  • Diffusion Planner: Diffusion-based planning for constrained deformable object manipulation.
  • Simple Robotic Trajectory Optimization: Single- and multiple-shooting DDP with constraints for motion planning of dynamical systems.
  • Gaussian Processes for Robot: Modeling robot dynamics with Gaussian processes.
  • Hopping Robot: 2D hybrid-dynamics simulation of a hopping robot with a Raibert controller for forward velocity and hopping height.
  • EziTrain: Python utilities that simplify machine learning model training.
